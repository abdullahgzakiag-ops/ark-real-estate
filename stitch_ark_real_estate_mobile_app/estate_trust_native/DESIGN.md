## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium, authoritative, and deeply reassuring presence tailored specifically for high-stakes property transactions, leasing, and asset management in Pakistan. Real estate decisions represent significant life and capital investments; thus, the emotional profile balances institutional stability with polished modern utility. 

The aesthetic synthesizes **Corporate Modern** with **Refined Editorial Craft**. It replaces loud consumer-tech tropes with deep architectural naval tones, crisp white surfaces, and deliberate, restrained champagne gold accents. The design communicates unyielding integrity, clarity of legal and financial records, and effortless navigation across diverse demographics—from institutional investors to everyday home seekers across major metropolitan areas.

Key emotional attributes:
- **Institutional Weight & Stability:** Grounded by authoritative deep naval blues that evoke established financial and property custodianship.
- **Translucent Clarity:** Crisp contrast ratios and disciplined layout structures ensure complex property deeds, tenancy contracts, and PKR payment milestones are instantly comprehended.
- **Restrained Prestige:** Warm gold and champagne metallics are deployed solely as accents for verified tiers, premium listings, and milestone actions, preventing visual fatigue.
- **Bi-directional Inclusivity:** Architecture and visual balance treat both Left-to-Right (English) and Right-to-Left (Urdu) typographic workflows with equivalent structural gravity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of financial permanence through a calibrated contrast hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#0B1E36`):** Deep Navy. Forms the dominant brand anchor, applied across top application bars, high-prominence navigation anchors, primary action buttons, and dominant visual banners.
- **Secondary (`#1E3A5F`):** Slate Navy. Provides secondary hierarchy, interactive secondary surfaces, active tab states, and segmented controls.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C5A059`):** Restrained Champagne Gold. Reserved strictly for prestigious tiers, verified agent badges, signature property highlights, and bespoke focal points. Never used for large surface fills.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E293B`):** Deep Charcoal. High-contrast body copy and structural icons ensuring maximum readability in varied outdoor mobile lighting conditions.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#64748B`):** Slate Gray. Meta-information, supporting subtitles, table headers, and inactive icon states.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft Grayish-White. Ground canvas for native Android viewports, mitigating harsh glare and framing elevated components cleanly.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white for cards, sheets, bottom navigation surfaces, and modal dialogs.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - *Success / Verified (`#10B981`):* Subtle Emerald Green for "Active", "Rented", "Paid", and verified ownership stamps.
  - *Warning / Pending (`#F59E0B`):* Refined Amber for "Pending Approval", "Under Review", and audit holds.
  - *Critical / Overdue (`#E11D48`):* Crimson for "Overdue", tenancy alerts, or payment defaults.
  - *Neutral Badge (`#64748B`):* Muted slate for "Sold" or archived ledger states.

## Typography

Typography is anchored by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its balanced geometric clarity, open apertures, and exceptional legibility across dense Android mobile screens (360dp to 430dp widths).

Rules for implementation:
- **Currency & Financial Display:** PKR values must be expressed using tabular numbers with explicit spacing: e.g., `PKR 45,000,000` or abbreviated forms `PKR 4.5 Crore` / `PKR 85 Lac` for listing headers. Pair currency tokens strictly with `currency-display` or `headline-md` at 700 weight.
- **Urdu Script Harmony (RTL):** When rendering Urdu, the system pairs Noto Nastaliq Urdu or Noto Sans Arabic with adjusted line heights (+20% line-height baseline compensation) while maintaining exact point sizes to ensure balanced vertical margins.
- **Numerical Alignment:** All price breakdowns, square footage metrics (Marla, Kanal, Sq. Ft.), and payment schedules must enforce strict tabular numerical alignment to prevent jitter in vertical lists.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a strict **8dp native Android spacing grid**, with a 4dp baseline sub-grid for tight micro-alignments (such as icon-to-label offsets and badge padding).

- **Mobile Viewport Target:** Primary canvas calibrated for 360dp–430dp portrait mobile layouts.
- **Horizontal Margins:** Outer margin token (`margin`) defaults to `1rem` (16dp), ensuring content stays clear of Android system gestures, display cutouts, and physical bezel curvature.
- **Column Architecture:** Fluid single-column architecture for primary transaction flows and property cards, scaling to a 2-column masonry grid on larger folding devices or small tablets (>600dp width).
- **Touch Ergonomics:** All interactive touch elements (buttons, bottom bar tabs, chip filters, pagination targets) must guarantee a minimum bounding target of `48dp x 48dp` (3rem x 3rem hit-box) regardless of visual glyph size.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 
  - Section-to-section gap: `space-xl` (32dp).
  - Inter-card stack gap: `space-md` (16dp).
  - Intra-card content block gap: `space-sm` (8dp).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs **Tonal Layering with Soft Ambient Shadows**, moving away from standard Material Design harsh multi-layered drop shadows in favor of calm, high-end architectural depth.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Completely flat. Used for screen backgrounds, scaffold bases, and full-bleed scroll containers.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Feed Items):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface elevated by a dual ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.03)`. Finished with a subtle ghost border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdown Menus & Sticky Navigation):** `#FFFFFF` surface with elevated ambient occlusion: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.06), 0 10px 24px -3px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Bottom Sheets & Modal Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` anchored to viewport bottom with: `box-shadow: 0 -8px 24px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.12)`. Supported by a dimming backdrop overlay of `#0B1E36` at 40% opacity (`rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Rounded** shape model (`roundedness: 2`), applying a default corner radius of `16dp` (`1rem`) to prime listing cards, structural dialogs, and image hero containers. 

Implementation specifications:
- **Property & Summary Cards:** 16dp border radius (`rounded-lg`). Top image viewports within cards inherit matching top radii (16dp) with bottom radii clipped to zero.
- **Inputs & Action Buttons:** 12dp border radius (`0.75rem`) to create a secure, buttoned-down structural boundary that distinguishes actions from cards.
- **Chips & Badges:** Fully circular/pill radii (`9999px`) to create clear shape differentiation between content containers and operational metadata tags.
- **Bottom Sheets:** Top-left and top-right radii set to 24dp (`1.5rem`), with bottom corners flat against screen edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0B1E36`, text `#FFFFFF`, height `52dp`, border radius `12dp`. Active ripple/pressed state darkens to `#071322`. Minimum touch target enforced at 52dp.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #1E3A5F`, text `#1E3A5F`, height `52dp`, border radius `12dp`.
- **Accent (Featured / Schedule Inspection):** Background `#C5A059`, text `#0B1E36` (high-contrast deep navy), bold typography.

### Status Chips & Badges
Chips maintain a compact `28dp` height, pill radius, padding `4dp 12dp`, using semantic tinted backgrounds with matching bold text:
- **Active / Verified:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, left-aligned 6dp emerald dot or verification icon.
- **Pending Approval / Under Review:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, left-aligned amber clock icon.
- **Rented:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`.
- **Sold / Archived:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`.
- **Paid:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`.
- **Overdue:** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#BE123C`.

### Manual Payment Logging Disclosure Badge
A specialized compliance badge for Pakistan market transaction logging (e.g., direct bank transfer/cross cheque):
- Container: Border `1px dashed #CBD5E1`, background `#F8FAFC`, radius `12dp`, padding `12dp 16dp`.
- Glyph: Subtle slate shield or receipt line-icon (`#1E3A5F`).
- Body: `body-sm` typography emphasizing: *"Payment logged manually. Subject to verification by ARK Estate custodian before clearance."*

### Property Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, radius `16dp`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Aspect ratio 16:10 for image gallery previews, overlaid with price tag (`PKR` formatted) anchored bottom-left in LTR (bottom-right in RTL).
- Spec ribbon: Inline list using thin line icons for bed, bath, and land area (e.g., `5 Marla` / `1 Kanal` / `2,250 Sq. Ft.`).

### Input Fields
- Height `52dp`, radius `12dp`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Focused state transitions border to `#1E3A5F` with an ambient ring `2px solid rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.15)`.
- Clear support for RTL text inputs with automated cursor flipping and phone number masks pre-formatted for Pakistani numbers (`+92 3XX XXXXXXX`).

### Lists & Key-Value Ledgers
- Divider lines use subtle `#F1F5F9` lines with `0.5dp` height.
- Financial breakdown rows alternate labels in `#64748B` and right-aligned bold totals in `#1E293B`.
